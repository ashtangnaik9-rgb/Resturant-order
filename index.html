P2P Restaurant Orders

Works without a central backend. Uses WebRTC data channels for direct P2P messaging. Signaling is manual copy/paste (no server). Devices must be on same Wi‑Fi/Hotspot for best reliability.

Signaling (manual)

1) One device clicks Create Offer, copies the long text and shares it (copy/paste or QR) with the other device.
2) Other device pastes into the box below and clicks Create Answer. The first device then pastes the answer into the box and clicks Accept Answer (button will appear). This establishes a P2P connection.
